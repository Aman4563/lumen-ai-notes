# Part 23 — Technology Selection, End-to-End Capstone, and Interview Synthesis

This final Part integrates the curriculum. It is not a list of trendy tools: it
maps durable system responsibilities to representative technologies, requires a
small end-to-end training/post-training/serving system, and turns the evidence into
senior interview narratives.

## Chapters

1. [AI/ML technology reference and selection guide](01-technology-reference.md)
2. [End-to-end foundation-model systems capstone](02-end-to-end-capstone.md)
3. [Senior and domain interview synthesis](03-interview-synthesis.md)

## Prerequisites

Parts 1–14 are core. Choose relevant advanced Parts 15–22 for the role, but the
capstone expects at least data, training, fine-tuning, inference, and evaluation
literacy. No individual is expected to be a research scientist, kernel specialist,
distributed-systems engineer, safety researcher, and product lead simultaneously.

## Exit gate

You can select technologies from requirements, explain what changes if a tool is
replaced, build a reproducible vertical slice, quantify quality/latency/cost,
inject and recover from failures, defend safety/security/data choices, and lead a
senior design discussion with explicit assumptions and evidence.
