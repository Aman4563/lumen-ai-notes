# Part 22 — Evaluation, Safety, Security, and Interpretability

This part treats evaluation as a versioned measurement system and safety/security
as properties tested across the full deployment—not as a final benchmark number.

## Prerequisites and chapters

Complete Parts 1–12 and the relevant advanced model/system Parts 16–21.

1. [Evaluation, safety, security, privacy, and interpretability reference](01-evaluation-safety-interpretability.md)
2. [Evaluation design and statistical workbook](02-evaluation-statistics-workbook.md)
3. [Safety, security, privacy, and red-team laboratory](03-safety-security-redteam-lab.md)

## Exit gate

Design a benchmark with construct validity and contamination controls; compare
models with paired uncertainty and multiplicity awareness; audit human/model judges;
evaluate agents by trajectory/outcome; distinguish capability elicitation from
deployment policy; test safety, prompt/tool security, privacy, distribution shift,
and interpretability claims; and define evidence-backed release gates.
