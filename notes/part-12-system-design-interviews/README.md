## What distinguishes senior answers

An SDE-II answer builds a correct scalable component/system and handles common
failures. An SDE-III answer additionally shapes ambiguous requirements, identifies
the highest-leverage trade-off, plans migration/adoption, defines ownership/SLOs/
cost/governance, and evolves the system across teams and years.

## Final outcome

You should be able to lead a 45–60 minute design without memorized diagrams,
derive architecture from requirements, quantify bottlenecks, handle ML-specific
feedback/labels, and defend staged choices.
